The thesis

The purpose of the CRM is not to generate more activity. Its purpose is to convert activity into measurable outcomes, convert outcomes into evidence, and convert evidence into management decisions.

The system is not a contact database and not a telecalling app. A hospital receiving 100 leads and converting 50 does not need 150 leads to reach 70 conversions. It needs to know what happened to the other 50 — how many hit a price objection, how many never got a proper follow-up, how many booked and never visited, how many are still recoverable.

Only after the leak is measured should more advertising spend be considered.

What the system must answer

  1. Where are the leads coming from?
  2. What is happening to every lead after it enters the system?
  3. Why are some leads converting?
  4. Why are some leads not converting?
  5. What action should management take to increase conversions without blindly increasing advertising expenditure?

What actually needs building

The six new screens are not equally important. Ranked by how hard the thesis leans on each.

Two of these are blocked on schema before any interface can be drawn: there is no Communication entity and no Template entity, so channel, template, delivery, read and reply are not stored per message. The follow-up engine, by contrast, is the strongest part of the build — LeadFollowupPlan carries all four protocols and all seven suppression conditions as real fields.

  1. Communication Composer and Template Library A6 · S3

    The 48-hour alternating model is the centrepiece of the revised thesis. There is no template system and no send-time guard, and nothing stores which channel was last used or when — so the rotation cannot be enforced. Touches are logged after the fact rather than orchestrated.

  2. The seven-part remark is not enforced A3

    The rule the rest of the thesis leans on hardest. NewCall.jsx validates two free-text fields, feedback and remarks, as non-empty. Evidence links, remark-quality scoring and conversion diagnosis all read from a structure that is not being captured.

  3. Drill-Down Explorer L5

    The nine-level drill is how every management conclusion is meant to be reached, from "conversions fell" down to "two agents did not send the package comparison creative". No drill-down code exists in the application.

  4. Financial Counseling Desk O2

    The thesis's own worked example turns on four of seven price objectors never receiving counseling. Nothing currently tracks counseling coverage, so that failure is invisible today.

  5. Communication Performance M9

    Without it there is no way to prove the 48-hour model works, or to find the channel sequence and content that precede conversions.

  6. 15-Day Diagnostic Report L6

    The recurring management document the entire thesis exists to produce. A trend chart exists; the diagnostic report does not.

  7. Supporting infrastructure M8 · O4 · S2 · S5

    Escalation desk, recovery console, assignment rules and the audit log viewer. The underlying data is largely already captured — these surface it.

AI layer — transcription and analysis

Structured remarks are the hardest rule in the thesis to enforce, because they compete with call volume. An agent finishing a six-minute call does not want to fill seven fields — so remarks end up thin, or agents end up slow.

Transcription removes the trade-off. The call itself becomes the evidence, and the remark is drafted from it. The agent's job shifts from typing to confirming.

Call audio → consent gate
→ Soniox transcript · speaker labels · timestamps
→ redaction strip identifiers before egress
→ OpenAI extraction · classification · summary · drafting
→ CRM draft presented for agent confirmation
→ audit model · prompt version · accepted or edited

What it produces

A drafted seven-part remark; the objection classified against the fixed reason taxonomy; evidence for the eleven qualification factors, each with its supporting quote; a suggested non-conversion reason with a transcript citation; remark-versus-transcript fidelity as a coaching signal; a two-line call summary for the timeline; and narrative drafting for the 15-day report from aggregated metrics.

What it never does

No AI output writes to a lead record unconfirmed. No AI-set status, no AI-closed lead, no AI-sent message. Reason codes come from the configured taxonomy — the model selects, it does not invent. Every extracted claim carries a timestamp; an extraction without a citation is dropped rather than shown.

Two things to settle before any code. First, there is no telephony integration in the application today — Soniox needs an audio source, and wiring a softphone or PBX is a larger piece of work than either API. Second, call recordings of patients discussing symptoms and finances are sensitive health data: recording consent changes the call opening script, and identifiers should be stripped before anything leaves the building.
